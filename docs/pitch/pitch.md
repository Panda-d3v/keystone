# Keystone — Investor Pitch Script
**Target speaking time: 4 minutes, excluding demo**

## Slide 1 — Keystone
*Approximately 35 seconds*

*[Hold up your iPhone 16.]*

This is my iPhone 16.

It looks like one product, but behind it is a whole network of suppliers, parts, and manufacturing decisions.

Each decision was made for a reason: price, capacity, origin, tariffs.

But what happens when those reasons change?

A new tariff comes in. A supplier moves production. Another manufacturer becomes a better option.

When the world changes, is my manufacturer still the right one for me?

And if not, how quickly can I make a better decision?

## Slide 2 — The Problem
*Approximately 60 seconds*

Imagine a new tariff is announced this morning.

A business needs to know which orders are exposed and whether moving production would actually save money.

That sounds straightforward until you look at where the answers live.

Orders sit in purchasing systems. Prices are in quotes. Origin evidence is in documents and emails.

Procurement, trade and finance each have a different piece.

In a 2025 Thomson Reuters survey, **72%** of trade professionals said they were using or considering sourcing changes in response to tariffs.

In a separate 2026 Altana and AAEI survey, **76%** couldn't provide complete product information within 24 hours when asked.

And the U.S. Census counts **6,428 importing manufacturers** with 100 to 499 employees — a starting market to investigate, not guaranteed buyers.

So the question is: how do we combine these records to make a decision faster?

## Slide 3 — Our Solution
*Approximately 45 seconds*

Keystone is designed for exactly that.

**First**, detect changes in tariffs, manufacturing origin or supplier conditions.

**Second**, identify the affected products and open orders, then present the feasible alternatives.

**Third**, let the team respond: keep its supplier, renegotiate, move production, or switch — while comparing costs and qualifications, not just unit prices.

**Fourth**, keep a human in the loop. The recommendation is reviewed, supported by evidence, and approved before action.

AI helps extract documents and draft the recommendation. Rules calculate the numbers. People make the decision.

## Slide 4 — Business Model
*Approximately 35 seconds*

Now, this isn't a one-time tariff calculator.

Supplier prices change. Qualifications expire. Companies revisit these decisions again and again.

We start with U.S. industrial importers and offer a paid trial: **$5,000 for one month or $10,000 for two**.

Our proposed go-to-market starts with PERLab introducing an industrial client. That company pays for the trial and evaluates the results.

If it proves valuable, clients can choose an annual subscription — $24,000 for a medium deployment and $48,000 to $96,000 for a larger one.

Trials don't convert automatically.

We want recurring revenue to come from recurring value.

*Approximately 60 seconds*

Our proposal models operating break-even in Year 3.

By Year 5, the forecast has **79 active subscribers, about $2.61 million in annual recurring revenue, and around $806,000 in operating profit**.

Those aren't results we've achieved. They depend on customer acquisition, retention, and keeping delivery costs under control — and that's what we need to validate.

We're asking Kearney PERLab to partner with Keystone.

The immediate step is **$30,000 over eight weeks** to build and evaluate the MVP, with a product counterpart, trade expertise, and one authorised industrial case.

At the end, we'll have the evidence to discuss client deployment, licensing, or even acquisition.

The opportunity is to turn a tariff or supplier change into an informed, traceable decision — before the old decision costs a company money.
